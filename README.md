## <img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/-8B5CF6?style=flat-square" height="14"/> About

I'm **Floyd**, founder of **FIDEL** — a studio that builds high-end websites, AI agents, and automations for small and mid-sized businesses. I work the whole stack, but I care most about **craft**: clean architecture, considered motion, sharp typography, and performance that scores 100 in Lighthouse.

Day to day I design and ship real products end-to-end — static marketing sites with hand-built animation, a multi-tenant CMS on Supabase with row-level security, and LLM-powered agents that draft (never auto-send) on the client's behalf. I'm largely self-taught and I ship for paying clients.

**Open To**
- Freelance web & frontend work
- Interesting collaborations and open-source
- Anything at the intersection of design, code, and AI
